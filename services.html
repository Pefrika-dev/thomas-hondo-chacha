02How I work

A clear process. No guesswork.

Good outcomes come from a considered sequence, not a sprint towards the first visual idea. Here is how a project typically moves from conversation to handover.

Start a conversation
  1. 01

    Understand the problem

    We start with your goals, audience and constraints. Before building, we get clear on the problem that actually needs solving.

  2. 02

    Define the requirements

    We map the content, features and what the first version needs to do. The scope becomes explicit, so expectations stay aligned.

  3. 03

    Design the experience

    I shape the layout and interface flow, then test decisions around structure, language and interaction before development.

  4. 04

    Develop the solution

    I build with clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript, paying attention to responsive behaviour, accessibility and performance.

  5. 05

    Test and refine

    I check the work across screen sizes, exercise forms and links, and fix what can be clearer, faster or more resilient.

  6. 06

    Prepare the handover

    You receive the code, supporting notes and what is needed to run and maintain the site with confidence.

04Pricing questions

Straight answers about cost and timing.

Is KSh 25,000 the final price?

It is the starting price for the Starter package: four to five pages with a clear scope. More pages, extra features or content work change the figure. You receive a written scope and a final price before any work begins.

What is not included in the package price?

Domain and hosting (KSh 10,000 a year), copywriting (KSh 3,000 per page), a logo or basic brand kit (KSh 15,000), M-Pesa integration outside the Commerce package (KSh 30,000), a booking system (KSh 25,000) and rush delivery under two weeks (+25%).

How do payments work?

50% deposit to start and 50% before the site goes live. Projects above KSh 100,000 are split 40%, 30% and 30%. A 5% discount applies when the full amount is paid upfront.

How long does a website take?

Starter about two weeks, Business four to six weeks and Commerce six to eight weeks, counted from the day your content and access are ready. Custom work is scheduled after the brief.

What does ongoing support cost?

The care plan is KSh 5,000 a month for updates, backups, monitoring and small edits. Every package also includes a support period after launch.

Do you work with businesses outside Migori?

Yes. Work is done remotely across Kenya, with updates on WhatsApp, calls or email. Handover and documentation are included either way.
