# Outreach pack — approved 2026-11-19

Owner approval for outreach was given on **2026-11-19**. This pack is the approved
sequence, the exact messages, and the log. It is not automation: every message is sent by
Thomas from his own WhatsApp or email account, one business at a time.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Send from Thomas's own accounts.** No bulk sender, no SMS gateway, no purchased lists,
   no third-party WhatsApp tools.
2. **One message per business per attempt.** Never forward the same text to a group, never
   copy-paste two businesses into one thread.
3. **Verified contacts only.** Numbers and emails labelled *unverified* or *placeholder* in
   `PROSPECTS.md` are out of scope until independently verified.
4. **No invented claims.** Every message below refers only to work that actually exists.
   Do not add "we already have 50 clients" or similar.
5. **One clear ask.** A short call or permission to send a link. Nothing else.
6. **Opt-out is final.** "Not interested", "stop", or silence after two follow-ups means the
   lead is closed. No third attempt.
7. **Log before moving on.** Update `outreach/tracker.csv` the same day. A lead that is not
   in the tracker did not happen.
8. **No prices until the scope is agreed.** The published packages are anchors; the written
   quote comes after the conversation.

## Sending window

- Monday to Friday, **09:00–11:30** and **14:00–16:30** East Africa Time.
- Maximum **5 first messages per day**. Quality beats volume; this is a one-person business,
  not a campaign.
- No messages on Sunday.

## What to record

For every send: date, channel, exact message variant used, reply, and the next action date.
For every reply: what they asked for, the price discussion, and whether it became a quote.
Only these records may be used to claim a reply rate or a win.
